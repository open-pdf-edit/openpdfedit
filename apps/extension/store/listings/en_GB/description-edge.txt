Turn your browser into a private PDF editor and reader. Organise, edit and sign PDF files locally, with a built-in text editor and search tools - no files ever leave your device.

FEATURES

• Read and annotate PDFs
• Edit and move text
• Reposition images
• Fill in forms and add signatures
• Permanently redact sensitive content
• Merge, split and reorder pages
• Password-protect and compress PDFs
• Compare documents
• Export to Markdown or plain text
• Undo and redo edits

All core features are free and require no account. Watermarking is an optional Supporter tool, unlocked with a one-time payment - no subscription, time limits or usage caps.

Requires Edge 103 or newer, or another modern Chromium-based browser.

Desktop versions are available for Windows and macOS.

GitHub: https://github.com/open-pdf-edit/OpenPdfEdit/
