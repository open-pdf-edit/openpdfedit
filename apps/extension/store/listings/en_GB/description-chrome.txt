OpenPdfEdit turns Chrome into a full PDF editor and reader - read, edit and organise PDF pages entirely on your device, powered by PDFium and Rust compiled to WebAssembly. Nothing is uploaded to any server.

FEATURES

• Read and annotate PDFs
• Edit and move text
• Reposition images
• Fill in forms and add signatures
• Permanently redact sensitive content
• Merge, split and reorder pages
• Password-protect and compress PDFs
• Compare documents
• Export to Markdown or plain text
• Undo and redo edits

All core features are free and require no account. Watermarking and OCR are optional Supporter tools, unlocked together with a one-time payment - no subscription, time limits or usage caps.

Requires Chrome 103 or newer, or another modern Chromium-based browser.

Desktop versions are available for Windows and macOS.

GitHub: https://github.com/open-pdf-edit/OpenPdfEdit/
